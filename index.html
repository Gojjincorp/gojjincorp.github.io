FAQ

Good to know.

Can we start without a detailed specification?

Yes. We can start with your goals and constraints, then define requirements and agree on a scope.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Yes. We start by reviewing the system and the changes you need. That helps us identify what to improve, extend, or integrate.

How do you estimate cost and timing?

Both depend on the scope, the integrations involved, and the condition of your existing systems. We can give you a clearer estimate after discussing the project.

What happens after launch?

We can support the system after launch, review feedback with you, and plan the next round of improvements.
